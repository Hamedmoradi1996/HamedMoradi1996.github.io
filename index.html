A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Research with a focus on the built environment.

I am a building-science researcher with an academic background in architectural engineering and architecture and energy. My interests include computational fluid dynamics, building energy performance, urban microclimates, and machine learning for environmental prediction.

I am particularly interested in computational methods that make environmental analysis faster and more useful during early-stage design, while maintaining a clear connection to physical modelling and validation.
